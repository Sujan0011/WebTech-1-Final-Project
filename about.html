About the yard

A Chunikhel scrap business that prepares plastic for recycling.

Nikhil Suppliers buys plastic bottle scrap in Budhanilkantha-11, Chunikhel. Bottles are first pressed in a baler into a compact, box-shaped bale. A shredder then cuts them into very small flakes.

The flakes go through a washing machine. Workers spread the clean flakes in the field to dry, then pack them. Packed flakes are what we sell to recycling buyers.

A blue pickup does the transport. It collects plastic bottles and takes the washed flakes to recycling buyers. Call 9827377237 to arrange a pickup or a delivery.
