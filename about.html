About the yard

A Chunikhel yard that prepares plastic for another life.

Nikhil Suppliers buys plastic bottle scrap in Budhanilkantha-11, Chunikhel. Bottles are pressed in a baler into a compact bale. A shredder then cuts them into very small flakes.

Those flakes go through a washing machine. Workers spread the clean pieces in the field until they are dry, then pack them. Packed, dry flakes are what we sell to recycling buyers.

A blue pickup does the road work. It collects bottles when a load is ready and takes washed flakes out to buyers. Call 9827377237 before a large pickup or a delivery.
